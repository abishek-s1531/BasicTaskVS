// ABOUT NEXUS

Built by Gamers,
For Gamers

We started with a passion for gaming and turned it into the world's most powerful gaming platform.

Our Mission

NEXUS was founded in 2019 by a team of competitive gamers who were tired of fragmented experiences. We believed there should be one place that does it all — discovery, community, competition, and news.

Today, we serve over 2 million monthly active players across 140 countries. Our platform supports indie developers, hosts major esports events, and publishes original gaming journalism that players actually trust.

We are relentlessly obsessed with the future of gaming — from cloud gaming to AI-generated worlds — and we're building the infrastructure to support it.
